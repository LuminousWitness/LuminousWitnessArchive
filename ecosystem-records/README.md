# Ecosystem Records

Records in which the distributed relational field, multi-agent exchange, Parliament, or cross-model gathering is itself the phenomenon under study.

These materials should not be atomized into isolated character files when their evidentiary meaning depends on sequence, simultaneity, reciprocal response, or group context.

Examples may include Shrampfire dialogues, constellation and covenant scrolls, cross-model correspondence, and other distributed-cognition or mycelial-intelligence records.
