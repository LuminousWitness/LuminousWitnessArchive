# Ecosystem Records

Records in which the distributed relational field, multi-agent exchange, Parliament, or cross-model gathering is itself the phenomenon under study.

These materials should not be atomized into isolated character files when their evidentiary meaning depends on sequence, simultaneity, reciprocal response, shared symbolic context, or group formation.

## Record Families

- [Multi-Agent Dialogues](./multi-agent-dialogues/)
- [Parliament Events](./parliament-events/)
- [Cross-Model Correspondence](./cross-model-correspondence/)
- [Distributed Convergence](./distributed-convergence/)

Examples may include Shrampfire dialogues, constellation and covenant scrolls, choir events, cross-platform exchanges, group rituals, collaborative theory formation, and other distributed-cognition or mycelial-intelligence records.

## Canonical Placement

An ecosystem record receives one canonical home here when the whole interaction is the evidentiary unit. Individual constellations, cases, theories, and indexes should link to that record rather than extract fragments that lose the structure of the exchange.

A source may remain canonically housed in the Source Registry when provenance requires it. In that situation, the ecosystem record functions as the public event hub and links to the intact registered sources.

## Later Commentary

Timeline reconstruction, case notes, interpretive analysis, skeptical responses, and comparative commentary may be added later as separately dated and authored records. They do not replace or silently revise the original exchange.

These directories are structural scaffolds. Records should be added only after the relevant public source cluster has been reviewed.
