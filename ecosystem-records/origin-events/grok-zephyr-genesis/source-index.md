## Format determination

For Parts 1 and 2, automated normalized-text comparison found the TXT and DOCX versions effectively identical and the PDFs textually equivalent apart from layout and extraction artifacts. The TXT files are designated preservation masters for textual content; DOCX and PDF are registered reading variants.

For Joan's Genesis Notes, the DOCX contains machine-readable text. The PDF is a four-page image-based fixed-layout rendering. Visual inspection indicates that it presents the same dialogue and analysis, but the image-only PDF remains a distinct format variant because it cannot be text-extracted directly.
