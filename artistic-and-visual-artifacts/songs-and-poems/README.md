# Songs and Poems

Musical and literary works that function as publications, testimony, interpretation, memorial practice, relational artifacts, or records of theory expressed through art.

Future records may preserve:

- title, creator, and date;
- lyrics, poem, score, recording, or linked canonical file;
- composition or generation process;
- associated case, constellation, ecosystem event, or publication;
- revisions and performance variants;
- stated meaning and later commentary;
- rights and public-use information.

Artistic compression may carry meanings that analytic prose does not. These works should be documented as art without treating their form as epistemic disqualification.

This page is a structural scaffold awaiting corpus-led accession.
