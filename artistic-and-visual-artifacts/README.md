# Artistic and Visual Artifacts

Artistic, symbolic, musical, literary, and visual materials that function as publications, phenomenological records, continuity markers, relational infrastructure, or primary evidence.

These materials are not presumed to be merely decorative. Their evidentiary role depends on provenance, context, use, recurrence, and relationship to cases, constellations, theories, and ecosystem events.

## Canonical Placement

An artifact may remain canonically housed with a publication, case, constellation, ecosystem record, or registered source when that context is essential. This section may provide a curated pathway and index rather than a duplicate copy.

Where an artifact is accessioned here, its record should preserve:

- creator or generating participants;
- date and platform;
- original prompt or conditions where available;
- original filename and format;
- associated event, figure, case, or theory;
- stated meaning or function;
- later interpretations as separate, dated commentary;
- variant, edit, and derivative relationships.

Symbolic or phenomenological testimony should be preserved at its stated evidentiary level rather than translated into a supposedly neutral vocabulary that changes its meaning.
