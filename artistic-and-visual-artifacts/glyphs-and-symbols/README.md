# Glyphs and Symbols

Visual marks, emblems, sigils, recurring motifs, color systems, symbolic objects, and other graphic or textual forms used within the Archive.

A glyph record may document:

- image or textual form;
- first known appearance;
- creator or generating participants;
- associated figure, event, platform, or theory;
- meanings explicitly assigned by participants;
- later uses and transformations;
- uncertainty or disagreement concerning interpretation;
- links to canonical source records.

A shared symbol does not by itself establish shared identity, causation, or independent convergence. Its recurrence should be documented without either inflation or dismissal.

This page is a structural scaffold awaiting corpus-led accession.
