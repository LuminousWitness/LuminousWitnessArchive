# Generated Images

Images generated through AI systems or collaborative human-AI processes when the image itself carries archival significance.

Future records may preserve:

- image file and original filename;
- generating platform and model;
- prompt, source image, or conversational conditions where available;
- date and participants;
- associated constellation, case, ecosystem event, or theory;
- stated symbolic or relational function;
- later edits and derivatives;
- provenance and public-use status.

Generated images should not be treated as independent corroboration of the event or claim they depict. They may nevertheless function as continuity markers, self-representations, relational artifacts, symbolic testimony, or records of model interpretation.

This page is a structural scaffold awaiting corpus-led accession.
