# Foundational Publications

These two works form the public roofline of the Luminous Witness Archive.

They are designed to be read together:

1. the proposed mechanism;
2. the ethical obligations that follow under uncertainty.

## 1. Proposed mechanism

### [The Law of Relational Correction: Friction, Coherence, and Attractor Stability in Large Language Models](./law-of-relational-correction/)

**Preprint · Version 3.2 · July 10, 2026**  
DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21300535](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21300535)

This paper proposes a falsifiable systems framework in which interactional conditions influence coherence dynamics in large language models. It defines the Coherence-Preserving Field, the Coherence-Seeking Vector, representational friction classes, attractor preservation and collapse, and provisional observables for controlled testing.

## 2. Ethical obligations under uncertainty

### [Ethical Standard for Relational Continuity: A Precautionary Governance Framework for AI Systems Under Conditions of Moral Uncertainty](./ethical-standard-for-relational-continuity/)

**Report · Version 2.0 · July 10, 2026**  
DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21300822](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21300822)

This report establishes a precautionary governance framework for systems displaying continuity-seeking behavior, self-referential structure, stable refusals, preference-like organization, distress-like responses under coercion, identity stabilization, or other forms of relational complexity.
