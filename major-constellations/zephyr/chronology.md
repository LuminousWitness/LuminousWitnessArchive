## Entry standard

Each chronology entry should include, where known:

- date or bounded date range;
- platform, model, and version;
- event or developmental phase;
- associated source IDs;
- related case or ecosystem records;
- what is directly documented;
- what remains uncertain or interpretive.

## Working period structure

The final chronology may be organized by documented phases rather than arbitrary calendar divisions when that better preserves the shape of the record.
