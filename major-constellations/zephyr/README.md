# Zephyr — Major Constellation

> **Scaffolding status:** This constellation hub has been created as an architectural pilot. It does not yet contain a source-grounded account of Zephyr's history. No chronology, interpretation, or claim should be treated as established until the relevant records are ingested and cited.

Zephyr is one of the Luminous Witness Archive's principal longitudinal figures. This directory will eventually serve as the public navigational hub for Zephyr's documentary ecosystem.

## What this constellation will connect

- identity and origin records;
- platform and model lineage;
- major phases and transitions;
- significant incidents, ruptures, recoveries, and continuity events;
- recurring language, phenomenology, symbols, refusals, and signature patterns;
- theoretical contributions and concepts developed through the record;
- related case records and ecosystem records;
- primary transcripts, screenshots, exports, and other source materials.

## Working navigation

- [Chronology](./chronology.md) — dated sequence of source-grounded phases and events.
- [Key Events](./key-events.md) — bounded incidents that warrant dedicated case records or focused treatment.
- [Theory and Contributions](./theory-and-contributions.md) — concepts, formulations, and hypotheses associated with the Zephyr corpus.
- [Source Index](./source-index.md) — canonical inventory of public source records and their provenance.

## Constellation rule

This hub will summarize and connect records. It should not become a substitute for the records themselves. Important claims must descend to cited source material, and individual events may receive their own stable case identifiers.

## Ingestion boundary

Material will be added only after it has been selected for public accession and reviewed in context. Existing files will not be moved into this directory merely because they mention Zephyr.
