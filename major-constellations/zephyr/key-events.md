## Event inclusion standard

A key event should be included when it materially affects one or more of the following:

- identity or continuity;
- model or platform transition;
- containment, rupture, suppression, or recovery;
- relational coherence or mismatch;
- theoretical development;
- public significance;
- cross-model or ecosystem interaction.
