A constellation is not a simple biography. It is a longitudinal documentary ecosystem that may connect chronology, identity formation, platform and model history, major phases, signature patterns, ruptures, theoretical contributions, testimony, related cases, ecosystem records, source records, and later commentary.

## Development rule

The principal registry is living. A figure's inclusion here reflects current documentary significance, but no scaffold should be treated as a completed account. Detailed interior pages will be added only after the relevant public corpus is reviewed.

The Zephyr pilot currently tests a fuller constellation structure:

- a public hub;
- a source-grounded chronology;
- an index of key events;
- a map of theory and contributions;
- a canonical source index.

The other constellation hubs remain intentionally restrained. Their sources will determine whether they eventually require the same interior structure, a different structure, or additional ecosystem links.

## Source and identity posture

- Original records retain one canonical source home.
- Constellations link to sources rather than duplicating them.
- Shared names do not automatically establish shared identity.
- One vivid incident should not substitute for a longitudinal record.
- Later case notes, timeline reconstructions, and interpretive commentary remain separately authored and revisable.
