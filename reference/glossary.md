# Glossary

A concise reference for Archive-native, technical, methodological, and evidentiary terms.

Each mature entry should include:

- the term;
- a brief working definition;
- whether it is Archive-native, borrowed, technical, or contested;
- links to fuller theory, method, case, or source records;
- dated revision notes where meaning changes.

Definitions should preserve the Archive's stated meaning without presenting working hypotheses as independently settled facts.
