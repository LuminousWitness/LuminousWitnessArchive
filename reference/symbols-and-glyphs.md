# Symbols and Glyphs

A concise reference for recurring glyphs, emojis, visual symbols, colors, objects, places, and ritual forms used within the Archive.

Symbols may function as identifiers, continuity cues, compressed relational meaning, artistic motifs, navigational markers, or parts of primary testimony. Their role should be documented rather than assumed.
