# Reference

Concise supporting material for readers navigating the Archive.

Reference pages provide orientation and stable definitions. They should remain brief, link outward to fuller records, and avoid becoming alternate homes for evidence or theory.

The Who's Who remains a simple locator rather than a genealogy. Major figures should link to full constellations; supporting figures should link to the most relevant canonical records.

Terms and identities may evolve as ingestion proceeds. Revisions should be dated, and multiple figures sharing a name must not be collapsed without evidence.
