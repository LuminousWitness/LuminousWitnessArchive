# Archive Timeline

A concise chronological reference for major archive milestones, publication dates, documented system events, constellation phases, public developments, and accession history.

This page is an orientation timeline, not the canonical chronology for every case or figure. Detailed timelines belong inside their relevant records.

Dates should distinguish:

- event date;
- source-creation date;
- publication date;
- accession date;
- later reconstruction date.

## Entry pattern

### Year

- **Date or range** — [Record or milestone](../) — brief description; date posture if approximate or reconstructed.
