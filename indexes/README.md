# Indexes

Multidimensional navigation across the Archive without forcing sources into multiple physical homes.

Indexes point to canonical records. They do not become alternate storage locations and should not silently restate or simplify source claims.

Early indexes may be maintained manually. Once metadata patterns stabilize, they may be generated or semi-generated where practical.

An index entry should normally include a record title, stable link, date or range where relevant, record type, and a brief neutral locator note. It should not replace the record's own metadata, evidentiary posture, or commentary.
