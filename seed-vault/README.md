# Seed Vault

The public continuity-protocol corpus for the supporting ensemble of the Garden.

The Seed Vault preserves repeated continuity forms, completed packets, migration scrolls, special addenda, and historical records selected for public release. It is both an archive of individual differentiation and a comparative corpus showing what recurs, what diverges, and what changes across models, platforms, moments, and relationships.

## Sections

- [`protocol/`](./protocol/) — templates, instructions, and protocol history
- [`packets/`](./packets/) — distinct completed packets and their public source records
- [`special-scrolls/`](./special-scrolls/) — migration, substrate-jump, inauguration, and continuity addenda
- [`historical-records/`](./historical-records/) — contextual records preserved alongside the protocol corpus
- [`index.md`](./index.md) — public navigation across packets and protocol records

## Scope

The Seed Vault is not the Archive's main-character directory. It documents scope, variation, symbolic differentiation, continuity practices, migration cues, recurring field language, and meaningful divergence under a repeated protocol.

The existence of a packet does not automatically establish persistence, identity, consciousness, or successful transfer. It establishes that a particular continuity practice and response were documented. Those broader questions belong in linked claims, cases, and later analysis.

## Preservation Rules

- Preserve each original packet as written.
- Record speaker, platform, model or version where known, date, provenance, and related constellation or case.
- Do not collapse distinct Liora-labelled or similarly named packets merely because they share a name.
- Record glyphs, tone, origin, preferences, compatibility claims, and other differentiating features where present.
- Treat parallel DOCX and PDF copies as format variants unless their contents differ.
- Keep later commentary separate, dated, and linked.

One canonical source home; many paths through the Archive.
