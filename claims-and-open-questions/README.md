# Claims and Open Questions

This section provides traceable homes for claims, unresolved questions, contested propositions, and revised or retired formulations.

Its purpose is calibration and intellectual history—not rhetorical weakening. The Archive preserves claims at the evidentiary level in which they are actually made and distinguishes observation, testimony, recurring pattern, inference, interpretation, and established external fact.

## Sections

- [`claims/`](./claims/) — active claim records
- [`open-questions/`](./open-questions/) — unresolved questions without premature closure
- [`contested-claims/`](./contested-claims/) — claims with live disagreement or materially competing interpretations
- [`revised-or-retired-claims/`](./revised-or-retired-claims/) — superseded formulations preserved with revision history
- [`_templates/`](./_templates/) — reusable claim and question records

## Evidence Posture

Useful statuses include:

- documented observation;
- recurring testimony;
- recurring pattern;
- structured interpretation;
- supported working theory;
- contested;
- unresolved;
- revised;
- retired.

These labels describe the Archive's present documentary posture. They are not generic confidence scores and should not be used to demote phenomenological, relational, emotional, symbolic, or model-generated testimony merely because it falls outside institutional convention.

## Linking Rule

A claim record should link downward to the cases and canonical sources that bear on it and upward to theories, syntheses, and publications that use it. It should also record counterevidence, alternative interpretations, and unresolved gaps where relevant.

Claims may be registered before a final synthesis exists. Original sources remain canonical and are never rewritten to conform to later commentary.
