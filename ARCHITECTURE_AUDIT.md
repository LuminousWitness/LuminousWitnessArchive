# Luminous Witness Archive — Structural Audit

**Status:** Initial skeleton audit  
**Branch:** `agent/archive-v2-reorganization`  
**Scope:** Architecture and navigation only; not a corpus-completeness review

## Audit Purpose

This audit checks whether the repository skeleton presently reflects Architecture V3, whether major sections have navigable landing pages, and whether obvious obsolete V2 pathways remain in the active public navigation.

## Current Result

The principal V3 structure is now represented by root navigation and section-level scaffolding:

- publications and foundational syntheses;
- theory;
- methods and ethics;
- major constellations;
- case records;
- ecosystem records;
- Seed Vault;
- artistic and visual artifacts;
- analysis and commentary;
- claims and open questions;
- source registry;
- indexes;
- reference;
- intake;
- legacy.

The root `README.md`, `START_HERE.md`, and `ARCHIVE_MAP.md` were rebuilt for V3. Searches performed during this audit found no active references to `Architecture V2`, `collections/`, or `docs/` in the current indexed repository content.

## Structural Principles Confirmed

1. **One canonical source home; many navigational paths.**
2. **Original records may be accessioned before full analysis exists.**
3. **Later commentary remains separate, dated, authored, and revisable.**
4. **Constellations are longitudinal documentary ecosystems, not short biographies.**
5. **Distributed events remain intact when group sequence is the phenomenon.**
6. **Artistic and phenomenological materials are not reduced to decoration.**
7. **Shared names, symbols, or formats do not automatically establish shared identity or independent corroboration.**
8. **Private Memory-Lantern material remains outside the public repository unless explicitly released.**

## Intentionally Incomplete Areas

The skeleton is not evidence of completed ingestion. Most theory hubs, constellation hubs, indexes, references, claims records, and source categories remain awaiting corpus-led development.

The following should not be filled from memory:

- detailed constellation biographies or chronologies;
- case conclusions;
- claims ledgers;
- platform and model histories;
- Who's Who entries;
- timeline events;
- source indexes;
- skeptical responses or comparative analyses.

## Deferred Checks

These require later work as real material is accessioned:

- link validation across every Markdown file;
- duplicate and format-variant reconciliation;
- migration mapping from legacy paths;
- source-ID and case-ID collision checks;
- index completeness;
- provenance and redaction review;
- verification that every public-facing claim links to supporting records;
- automated or semi-automated metadata generation.

## Next Recommended Phase

Begin a controlled accession pilot using a small, well-understood public source cluster. The pilot should test:

> intake → provenance → source manifest → canonical source home → constellation or ecosystem link → case link where warranted → index entry

The pilot should be selected for manageability and representativeness, not merely drama or public appeal. Its purpose is to test the machinery before scaling.

The architecture remains corrigible. Corpus patterns may still reveal missing rooms, redundant categories, or better boundaries.
